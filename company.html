Our Story

Built on Trust, Not Pressure

AutoElite Motors started in 1998 as a single-lot dealership on Detroit's east side, built on a simple idea: car buying shouldn't feel like a fight. No pushy sales tactics, no hidden fees — just honest people helping other people find a car they'll actually love.

Today we're still family-owned and still run by many of the same people who opened our doors nearly three decades ago. Every vehicle on our lot passes the same 150-point inspection we started with, and every deal is still handled by a real person who'll pick up the phone.
